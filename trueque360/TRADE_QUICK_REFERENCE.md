# 🚀 Quick Reference - Trade System Updates\n\n## What Changed?\n\n✨ **Users can now propose trades WITHOUT publishing articles first**\n\n### Before\n- Only published articles could be traded\n- Users had to create formal listings first\n- Time-consuming process\n\n### After  \n- Trade with published articles (existing) OR\n- Trade with free-text descriptions (NEW)\n- Fast, flexible, intuitive\n\n---\n\n## For Users\n\n### To Propose a Trade\n\n1. Find an article/item to trade for\n2. Click \"Proponer Trueque\"\n3. Modal opens with 2 tabs:\n   - **📦 Seleccionar Artículo**: Pick from your published items\n   - **✍️ Describir Artículo**: Type what you're offering (NEW!)\n4. Make your selection/description\n5. Click \"Proponer Trueque\"\n\n### To View Trades\n\nGo to \"Mis Trueques\" tab:\n- See both received and sent proposals\n- Accept or Reject (for received)\n- See status of proposals\n\n**Display types**:\n- 📦 Article: Shows image + title\n- ✍️ Description: Shows text in dashed box\n\n---\n\n## For Developers\n\n### Key Files Modified\n\n```\nBackend:\n├── models/Trade.js (optional fields added)\n└── controllers/tradeController.js (flexible validation)\n\nFrontend:\n├── components/TradeProposal/TradeProposal.jsx (tabs added)\n├── components/TradeProposal/TradeProposal.css (tab styles)\n├── components/TradesTab/TradesTab.jsx (conditional rendering)\n└── components/TradesTab/TradesTab.css (description styles)\n```\n\n### API Changes\n\n**POST /api/trades** now accepts:\n```json\n{\n  \"receiverId\": \"user_id\",\n  \"proposerArticleId\": \"id or null\",\n  \"proposedItemDescription\": \"text or null\",\n  \"receiverArticleId\": \"id or null\",\n  \"requestedItemDescription\": \"text or null\"\n}\n```\n\n**Validation Rule**: Must provide at least ONE of (articleId OR description) for each side\n\n### Database Schema\n\nNew optional fields in Trade model:\n- `proposedItemDescription` (String, max 500, optional)\n- `requestedItemDescription` (String, max 500, optional)\n\nExisting fields now optional:\n- `proposerArticle` (was required)\n- `receiverArticle` (was required)\n\n---\n\n## Common Scenarios\n\n### Scenario 1: User has published articles\n```\nTradeProposal modal\n  ↓\n[📦 Seleccionar] [✍️ Describir]\n  ↓\nClick 📦 → Select
